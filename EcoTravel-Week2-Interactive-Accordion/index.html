Sustainable travel means exploring destinations while reducing environmental impact and supporting local communities. It can include responsible transportation, eco-friendly accommodations, and locally owned businesses.

Look for accommodations that use renewable energy, reduce water consumption, minimize waste, and support the surrounding community. Eco-certifications can also help identify responsible properties.
