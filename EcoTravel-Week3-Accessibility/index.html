Inclusive travel

Accessibility tips

Use these questions to make trip planning more considerate and practical.

Check accommodation and transport information in advance, contact providers with specific questions, and confirm details such as step-free entrances, accessible toilets and route conditions.

Interactive controls should be reachable with Tab, have a clear focus indicator, use native buttons or links where possible, and provide logical reading and interaction order.

Clear labels help people understand what controls do, including users of screen readers and people with cognitive or learning disabilities. Labels should describe the action or purpose without relying only on visual context.

About this project

Accessibility is part of good UX.
